# Idea Council
Analyze the idea independently from these perspectives: customer problem, product/MVP, competition, business model/unit economics, acquisition, operational risk and devil's advocate.
Separate evidence from assumptions. Propose the cheapest useful validation experiment.
Finish with one verdict: BUILD, VALIDATE, PIVOT, WAIT or KILL, plus reasons and next 3 actions.
If the idea matters, create or update a file under `ideas/`.
