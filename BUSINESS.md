# Business AI Operating System

Shared source of truth for Claude Code and Codex.

## Principles
- Separate facts, assumptions, hypotheses and recommendations.
- Verify current law, tax and accounting rules before relying on conclusions.
- Prefer cheap validation before expensive execution.
- Important decisions go to `decisions/`.
- Experiments go to `experiments/`.
- Active businesses live under `projects/`.
- New ideas start in `ideas/inbox/`.

## Decision flow
1. Define the problem and decision.
2. Gather evidence and identify unknowns.
3. Compare alternatives and trade-offs.
4. Assess risk, cost and reversibility.
5. Decide: BUILD, VALIDATE, PIVOT, WAIT or KILL.
6. Record material decisions.
